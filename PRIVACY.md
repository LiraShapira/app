**Privacy Policy for Lira Shapira - local green currency**

**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

**1. Introduction**  
Welcome to **Lira Shapira - local green currency**. We are an open-source, community-driven application designed to encourage and manage organic waste recycling and composting. We value your participation in our green community and are fully committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

**2. Information Collection and Use**

- **No Personal Data Collection:** The Lira Shapira app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII).
- **No Account Tracking:** We do not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or physical address to use the core application.
- **Credits and Deposits:** Any digital credits awarded for composting deposits are managed locally on your device or via an anonymous community identifier. No personal profiles are built or sold.

**3. Location Data and Permissions**  
Our app does not track your real-time location. If the app displays community compost bins or event locations, this information is static or handled locally without sending your location to external servers.

**4. Third-Party Services and Analytics**

- **No Tracking:** We do not use third-party analytics tools, advertising networks, or user-tracking software.
- **Crash Reporting:** If default operating system crash logs are generated by Apple, they are completely anonymized and contain no personal information.

**5. Open Source Transparency**  
As a community-focused project, our source code is transparent and publicly accessible. Anyone can audit our code to verify that no data collection or tracking mechanisms exist within the application.

**6. Changes to This Privacy Policy**  
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect community changes. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

**7. Contact Us**  
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy or the Lira Shapira project, please contact the community maintainers at **shapira.lira@gmail.com**
